Ohio Golf Club style guide

What the public site at ohiogolfclubindoor.com renders today, captured October 10, 2026 at 390, 768 and 1440 px. These are observations, not decisions. Every value is a hypothesis to check against the theme code.

The site is built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS. Most sizes, grays and shadows are Tailwind defaults. Only one theme variable exists, --color-primary: #ae1b22. The other brand colors are written as one-off values such as text-[#011E41] and bg-[#222].

Pages: home, team, events, contact-us, fall-league, carls-place. Privacy and terms returned 404. Full screenshots are in screenshots/, and the full audit is in audit.md.

Repeated patterns

  1. Section intro. A centered Montserrat heading (36 to 48px) and an 18px paragraph, then the content, then one centered red button.
  2. Alternating rows. Text and photo side by side, mirrored on the next row. Each row has a heading, a paragraph, an optional check list and one button.
  3. Hero recipe. Red pill badge, big heading, paragraph, one primary button. Only the home hero adds a secondary outline button.
  4. Photo with overlay. A black overlay or gradient behind white text, for heroes, tiles, team photos and the closing banner.
  5. Closing banner. A full-bleed photo with a centered heading and a large button, just before the footer.
  6. One CTA per section. The secondary path is usually a text link, not a second button.
  7. Background rhythm. Black and #121212 sections alternate. Cards use #222222 on dark, or white on #121212 for pricing.
  8. Fade in on scroll. Most blocks fade and rise over 600ms as they enter the viewport.

Inconsistencies

Ranked by how much they matter for building shared components.

  1. Colors bypass the theme. Only the red is a variable. Navy, the near blacks, the card charcoal, the light red and the star yellow are written inline, so they need names before they can become tokens.
  2. Five near-black surfaces: #000000, #0a0a0a, #121212, #1a1a1a and #222222. These could be three: page, raised section and card.
  3. Buttons that look alike but aren't the same. There are three filled sizes (one used only once), and one CTA has no hover and a different focus ring. Suggested: primary, outline and text variants, in medium (48px) and large (56px).
  4. Four card styles that look alike. Offering (12px, no border, centered), event (16px, bordered, left aligned), review (black, 12px) and testimonial (#222, italic). These could be one card with options.
  5. Seven corner radii: 4, 8, 10, 12, 16 and 28px, plus full. Suggested: 4 for inputs, 8 for controls, 12 and 16 for cards, full for pills.
  6. Twenty font sizes, eight of them off the Tailwind scale. Letter spacing has 28 values.
  7. Headings skip levels. Home, team and events go from h1 to h3. This affects search, which is in scope, and the heading component.
  8. Two reds. #e0474f appears only on the fall league page, alongside the brand red and four transparent versions of it.
  9. Decorative gradients are concentrated on two surfaces. They're easy to flatten.
  10. Uppercase is applied ad hoc. It should become a named text style (eyebrow or label).
